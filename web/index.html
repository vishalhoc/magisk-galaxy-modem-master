🎯 NR ARFCN & PCI Technical Details
NR ARFCN: Absolute Radio Frequency Channel Number. In 3GPP TS 38.104, ARFCN 634080 corresponds precisely to 3511.20 MHz (Mid-Band n78). Locking to this ARFCN forces the modem to prioritize the 100 MHz ultra-wide carrier.
NR PCI: Physical Cell Identity ($0 \le PCI \le 1007$, computed as $3 \times SSS + PSS$). Identifies the exact sector antenna on the cell tower. Locking/targeting a specific PCI stops the modem from ping-ponging between multiple antennas on cell edges, slashing latency jitter.
Hardware Band Select (*#2263#): Launches Samsung's official radio layer menu to select individual NR bands (n78/n28) directly into the modem's non-volatile memory.
🚀 5G NR Architecture (SA vs NSA & ENDC) SA Active

5G Standalone (SA Mode)
Connects directly to pure 5G Next-Gen Core (5GC) without 4G LTE anchor
5G Non-Standalone (NSA Mode)
Connects to 4G LTE EPC core with secondary 5G NR carrier attachment
E-UTRA-NR Dual Connectivity (ENDC)
Allows simultaneous transmission across 4G LTE and 5G NR carriers
🚀 5G NSA Mode (Option 3x) Deep Dive
What is 5G NSA? In Non-Standalone (NSA), the phone must attach to a primary 4G LTE anchor carrier (e.g. Band 3 or Band 40) for control signaling, while adding a secondary 5G NR carrier (e.g. Band n78) for high-speed user plane data via ENDC.
Operator Differences: Airtel 5G Plus is purely 5G NSA; it requires NSA & ENDC to function. Reliance Jio is purely 5G SA; it requires SA mode.
Underlying System Props: persist.vendor.radio.nr_sa_mode, nr_nsa_mode, and endc_enabled.
