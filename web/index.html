🧠 The 3 Locking Modalities Explained (3GPP Rel-15)
Option A (Band Whitelist): Restricts the transceiver strictly to selected frequency bands, eliminating unwanted handovers to low-speed fallback layers.
Option B (ARFCN Frequency Lock): Binds radio search to exact carrier channels (e.g. ARFCN 634080 for 100 MHz n78).
Option C (PCI Sector Lock): Binds connection to the exact physical tower sector antenna, eliminating latency jitter.
